## Measured Real Session Counts and Properties

These values are generated deterministically by `data.generator.generate_instance(PRESETS[name])`:

### 1. Small Preset (`small`)
* **Grid**: 5 days (Mon–Fri) $\times$ 6 slots (09:00–15:00), 30 time slots per week.
* **Resources**:
  * 4 Rooms (2 Lecture, 1 Computer Lab, 1 General Lab; capacities 40–80).
  * 3 Student Divisions (Divisions A, B, C; sizes 30–70).
  * 4 Teachers (T01–T04).
  * 6 Courses (C01–C06; 1 lab course with duration 2, 5 lectures with duration 1).
* **Total Weekly Sessions**: **45 sessions**
* **Total Slot-Hours**: **54 slot-hours**
* **Total Room-Slots Available**: $4 \times 30 = 120$ slot-hours
* **Actual Utilization**: $54 / 120 = \mathbf{45.0\%}$
* **Planted Solution**: Guaranteed feasible (passes independent `validate_timetable` with 0 violations).

## Seed Reproducibility
Every synthetic instance is constructed using `random.Random(cfg.seed)`. When identical `GeneratorConfig` configurations are supplied, the output instances and their planted solutions are strictly identical.
